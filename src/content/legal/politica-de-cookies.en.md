## What we use

We use storage necessary for the site to work, preferences for your language and filters, and analytics — the latter **only with your consent**, given in the cookie notice and withdrawable at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer. Your choice is stored in your browser (`enredarte-consent`, see table).

## First-party storage table

| Key | Type | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| `enredarte-consent` | localStorage | Remembers your analytics choice | Until you clear it |
| `enredarte-catalog-storage` | localStorage | Remembers your collection filters | Until you clear it |
| `enredarte-form-storage` | localStorage | Keeps your name/email draft | Until you clear it |
| `enredarte-last-artwork` | sessionStorage | Resumes your purchase if payment is interrupted | Session |
| `hero-entered` | sessionStorage | Skips replaying the hero animation | Session |

## Measurement

- **Own counter:** each artwork visit sends an anonymous ping to our server (no cookies). Sent only with your consent.
- **Google Analytics (`_ga`, `_ga_*`):** Google LLC cookies measuring visits and purchases (amount and currency, no personal data). Enabled only with your consent, lasting up to 24 months, per Google's settings. They involve a transfer to the United States. You can additionally opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on and withdraw consent via "Cookie settings".

## Third-party content

- **Videos (YouTube-nocookie, Vimeo):** playing a blog video may let the provider set its own cookies.
- **Outbound links:** Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp apply their own policies once you leave our site.

## How to manage them

Use the cookie notice or "Cookie settings" in the footer to change your choice, and your browser settings to clear storage. Rejecting analytics does not affect browsing or purchase. If you withdraw consent, we stop processing your measurement data from that moment; to request its deletion, exercise your ARCO rights under the Privacy Notice.
