## Data controller

EnredArte, based in Mexico City, Mexico, controls your personal data. Contact: info@enredarte.com and +52 624 176 4802.

## Data we collect

- **Purchase:** email address and chosen currency, to create your order and send you to payment.
- **Shipping:** receiver name and phone, country, state, city, postal code, neighborhood, street, exterior and interior numbers, cross streets, references, and delivery notes.
- **Measurement (only with your consent):** visits to rooms and works via our own first-party counter and Google Analytics.
- **Inquiries:** name, email, phone, and message content when you contact us by email, phone, or WhatsApp.

We never collect or store your card or bank details: payment is processed on Stripe's secure checkout page. We also collect no biometric data.

## Purposes

Your data is used solely to answer inquiries, process and deliver your purchase, and measure visits when you consent. **We do not use your data for marketing:** we send no newsletters or promotions.

## Processors and transfers

- **Stripe:** payment processor. At checkout you leave for their secure payment page, and Stripe handles your email and payment details under its own policy.
- **Google LLC (only with your consent):** web analytics. This involves a transfer to the United States. See the Cookie Policy.
- **DHL / FedEx:** receive your shipping details solely to deliver your insured artwork.

Once shared as described, each third party is responsible for its own processing under its policies.

## Retention

We keep your data for the duration of the commercial relationship and afterwards only for the periods required by applicable tax and legal obligations. Measurement data is retained as described in the Cookie Policy.

## Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection by writing to info@enredarte.com. Include your name, a reply channel, which right you exercise and over which data, and attach an ID. We respond within 20 business days of your complete request. If you consider your request unaddressed, you may contact INAI (www.inai.org.mx).
