## Why

Collectors can browse galleries and artworks, but artists are invisible as destinations: the nav's "Artistas" link points at a dead `#artistas` anchor, and no artist has a page. A dedicated artist page — their info, their works, and the galleries where they are active right now — makes the human behind each piece discoverable and deepens EnredArte's "curated by humans" positioning.

## What Changes

- Add a build-time artist detail page per artist, in both languages: `/artistas/<slug>` (Spanish) and `/en/artistas/<slug>` (English), generated by extending the existing `[...path].astro` catch-all `getStaticPaths()` and `COMPONENT_MAP` (no new route file).
- Render the artist's info from the API: photo, name, localized bio, birth/death years, location, email, website, and social links.
- Render the artist's artworks as a static editorial list reusing the gallery-page pattern: a featured `ImageBanner` followed by alternating `ImageRowCard`s, tagged with discipline/technique/theme labels. No filter UI on this page.
- Render the galleries where the artist is currently active (typically 1–3): galleries hosting at least one of the artist's artworks, deduplicated, restricted to `is_active`, ordered primary-first, shown as `ImageCard`s reusing `toSalaView`.
- Make the artist name on artwork detail pages a link to the artist's page (the entry point).
- Fix the nav anchors in `lib/nav.ts` (shared by Header and Footer) to real homepage section targets instead of dead `#obras`/`#salas`/`#artistas` placeholders.
- Add `locations` to the build-time site data (needed to resolve an artist's location name).
- Keep the `LangBtns` language switch on the artist slug, emit localized SEO metadata, and add the new i18n keys to both language files.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `artist-detail-page`: Build-time artist detail pages in both languages, rendering artist info, their artworks as a static editorial list, and their currently-active galleries, with a slug-preserving language switch and localized SEO metadata.

### Modified Capabilities
- `header-organism`: Navigation anchor targets change from UI-only placeholders to real homepage section anchors.
- `footer-organism`: Same nav anchor change, inherited through the shared `getNavLinks` helper.

## Impact

- `src/pages/[...path].astro` — artist paths added to `getStaticPaths`, `artist` entry in `COMPONENT_MAP`, `artistSlug` prop threading, localizedPaths + preloadImage branches.
- `src/components/pages/artista/ArtistPage.astro` — **new** page component (mirrors `GalleryPage` structure).

- `src/lib/i18n/utils.ts` — add `getLocalizedArtistPath`.
- `src/lib/nav.ts` — nav anchors point at real sections (`#salas-gallery`, `#artworks-collection`).
- `src/messages/es.json` + `src/messages/en.json` — new `global.artist.*` keys (must stay in parity for `validate-i18n`).
- `docs/component-dependencies.md` — ArtistPage tree added (docs sync requirement).
- Build-time backend dependency unchanged (same `buildSiteData` fetch).
